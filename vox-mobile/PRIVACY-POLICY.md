# VOX — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 2026-07-11
**App:** VOX (Android) — package `com.vox.mobile`
**Owner:** VOX

## 1. Overview
VOX is a voice, text, and community chat application. This policy explains what
data we collect, why, and how you can control it.

## 2. Data we collect
- **Account data:** email, username, password (hashed), optional profile picture and bio.
- **Social data:** servers you join, messages, direct messages, friends, and reactions.
- **Voice data:** real-time audio transmitted only while you are in a voice channel.
  Audio is relayed for the call and is not permanently stored on our servers.
- **Device data:** device model, OS version, and push notification tokens, used for
  delivery of notifications and crash diagnostics.
- **Usage data:** anonymous analytics about feature usage to improve the app.

## 3. How we use data
- To create and secure your account.
- To deliver messages, voice, and notifications in real time.
- To moderate content and enforce community guidelines.
- To diagnose crashes and improve reliability.

## 4. Data sharing
We do **not** sell your personal data. Data is shared only with:
- Other users in the servers/channels you choose to join.
- Service providers that power hosting, push notifications, and analytics, under
  contractual confidentiality.

## 5. Data retention
Messages and account data are retained while your account is active. You can delete
your account at any time from **Settings → Account**, which removes your personal data.

## 6. Security
Passwords are hashed; API traffic uses HTTPS/TLS. Voice channels are authenticated.

## 7. Children
VOX is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your jurisdiction) and
we do not knowingly collect their data.

## 8. Your rights
You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data by contacting
**privacy@vox.app**.

## 9. Changes
We will post changes to this policy and update the effective date.
