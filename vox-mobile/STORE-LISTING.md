## Full description
VOX is the all-in-one voice and text chat app for communities. Create or join
servers, hop into low-latency voice channels, send direct messages, and stay
connected with friends — all in a fast, dark, and modern interface.

Features:
- 🔊 Crystal-clear voice channels with mute, deafen, and speaking indicators
- 💬 Text channels, direct messages, reactions, and rich message history
- 🏠 Servers and channels with roles and server discovery
- 👥 Friends list, invites, and presence/status
- 🔔 Push notifications for messages and mentions
- 🔐 Two-factor authentication and secure login
- 🌙 Beautiful dark-mode-first design

Whether you're gaming, studying, or running a community, VOX keeps everyone in sync.
